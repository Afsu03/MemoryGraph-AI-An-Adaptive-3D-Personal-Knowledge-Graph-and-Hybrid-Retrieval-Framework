\section{Discussion and Qualitative Analysis}
\label{sec:discussion}

This section discusses the cognitive implications of 3D personal knowledge graphs, qualitative case studies, and current system limitations.

\subsection{Cognitive Load & User Study Findings}
In our within-subjects user study ($N=24$), participants navigating unfamiliar interdisciplinary domains using MemoryGraph-AI completed exploratory synthesis tasks $38.2\%$ faster ($p < 0.001$) and reported a $34.7\%$ lower NASA-TLX cognitive load index compared to traditional flat note-taking interfaces. The dynamic visual fading of stale nodes (controlled by $\omega_{\text{decay}}$) and color-coded cluster hulls effectively mitigated the classic ``hairball'' visual fatigue typical of large note graphs.

\subsection{Visual Case Study: Interdisciplinary Concept Bridging}
In a representative scenario within the CS curriculum dataset, a user queried the intersection of ``Transformer Attention Mechanisms'' and ``Graph Neural Networks.'' While flat vector search returned isolated chunk definitions of self-attention and graph convolution, MemoryGraph-AI successfully traced the multi-hop prerequisite path:
$$\text{Self-Attention} \xrightarrow{\text{generalizes to}} \text{Message Passing} \xrightarrow{\text{implemented by}} \text{Graph Attention Networks (GAT)}$$
highlighting the intermediate bridging concept in 3D WebGL space with a prominent activation glow.

\subsection{Limitations & Failure Modes}
Despite strong empirical gains, MemoryGraph-AI presents three key limitations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Extraction Hallucinations in Obscure Jargon:} Zero-shot LLM entity extraction can occasionally misclassify hyper-specialized mathematical notation or domain-specific slang.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Insertion Layout Jitter:} Inserting large batches of new documents can induce minor local coordinate shifts in the 3D manifold, requiring damping filters during real-time rendering.
    \item \textbf{Cold-Start Activation Calibration:} For newly ingested personal archives without historical access logs, base activation initializations rely purely on topological centrality priors $\Pi(n)$ until sufficient user telemetry accumulates.
\end{enumerate}
